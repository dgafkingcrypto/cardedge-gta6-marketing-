Gaming YouTube Titles and Thumbnails: A Planning Checklist

A good title makes one accurate promise; a thumbnail reinforces that promise. Neither needs fabricated leaks or misleading urgency.

Start with a viewer outcome

Write one sentence explaining what the viewer will learn. Draft a question-based title, a practical title and a comparison title. Use the Thumbnail & Title Lab ($12) to record ideas.

Make thumbnails readable

Choose one focal point, strong contrast and minimal text. Check legibility at phone size and use only authorized imagery.

Measure carefully

Track impressions, click-through rate and retention when available. The Analytics Kit ($19) organizes metrics, but no template guarantees views or revenue.
